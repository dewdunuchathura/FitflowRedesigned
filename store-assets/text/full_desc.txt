FitFlow – Your AI-Powered Fitness Companion

Take control of your fitness journey with FitFlow, the all-in-one app that combines AI-driven workout recommendations, smart nutrition tracking, progress analytics, and a motivating community — all in a clean, easy-to-use interface.

━━ KEY FEATURES ━━

🤖 AI Workout Coach
FitFlow's AI Workout screen analyses your fitness goals and workout history to generate personalised daily workout plans. From beginner bodyweight routines to intermediate full-body burns, every recommendation is tailored to you. Accept, adjust, or skip — you're always in control.

🏠 Smart Home Dashboard
Your Daily Flow dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of today's AI-recommended workout, calorie progress, completed workouts, nutrition score, and your current streak. Everything you need, right on the home screen.

🍎 Nutrition Tracker
Log meals, track calories, and monitor macronutrients with the Nutrition screen. Set dietary goals, track daily intake, and see how your nutrition supports your fitness targets.

📊 Progress Analytics
The Progress screen visualises your improvement over time with charts, streak tracking, and milestone celebrations. Watch your strength, endurance, and consistency grow week by week.

👥 Fitness Community
Join the Social Feed to share achievements, encourage others, and stay accountable. Visibility controls let you decide what to share. Report or block users who don't respect community standards.
