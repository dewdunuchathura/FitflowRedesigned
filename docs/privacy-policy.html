Privacy Policy

FitFlow – Your AI-Powered Fitness Companion

Effective Date: 2026-10-03  ·  Version 1.0

👋 Who We Are

FitFlow is a consumer fitness application developed as part of an academic project (IT3060 Human-Computer Interaction). The app is operated by AGDC Bandara ("we", "us", "our") via the GitHub repository dewdunuchathura/FitflowRedesigned.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency, security, and respect.

📋 Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account we collect your email address, display name, and an authentication credential managed through Firebase Authentication. We do not store raw passwords.

Health & Fitness Data

To power your personalised workouts we collect data you provide or that is generated within the app:

  • Workout history (exercise type, sets, reps, duration, calories burned)
  • Fitness goals (weight, strength, endurance, flexibility)
  • Body metrics you voluntarily enter (height, weight, age)
  • Workout completion streaks and progress indicators
Nutrition Data

When you use the Nutrition screen the app records:

  • Meals logged, calorie intake, and macronutrient targets
  • Food preferences and dietary restrictions you enter
Social & Community Activity

The Community (Social Feed) screen may collect:

  • Posts, comments, and reactions you create or submit
  • User-to-user interactions (likes, follows, shares)
  • Reports or blocks you initiate against other users
Device & Usage Data

We may automatically collect limited technical data including:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and session timestamps
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics (anonymised)

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to:

  • Provide and improve core app functionality (workouts, nutrition tracking, progress)
  • Generate personalised AI workout and nutrition recommendations
  • Display your progress history and streak statistics
  • Enable social features such as community posts and peer motivation
  • Send optional in-app notifications about workouts or goals
  • Debug crashes and improve performance
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising profiling, sell it to third parties, or share it with advertisers.

🤖 AI-Powered Recommendations

FitFlow uses AI to suggest workouts and nutritional plans. These suggestions are generated from your workout history, stated fitness goals, and usage patterns within the app.
  • You may accept, adjust, or skip any AI recommendation at any time.
  • AI recommendations are for general wellness purposes only and are not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to your exercise or dietary routine.
  • Your data is processed on-device or via a secure backend service. It is not used to train third-party AI models without your explicit consent.

👥 Social Features

FitFlow's Community screen allows users to share fitness achievements and interact with others. Please be aware that:

  • Posts you publish are visible to other FitFlow users. You control what you share.
  • You can adjust your visibility settings at any time in your profile.
  • You may report or block any other user who violates community guidelines.
  • We reserve the right to remove content that violates our terms or applicable law.

🔒 Storage, Security & Sharing

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure). We apply industry-standard safeguards including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
  • Role-based access controls limiting who can view your data
  • Regular security reviews as part of ongoing development

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Firebase / Google – authentication and database services (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Legal authorities – only when required by applicable law or court order

🗑️ Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the service.

  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address in the Contact section below.
  • Upon deletion, your personal data will be removed from active systems within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Anonymised and aggregated usage statistics may be retained indefinitely.

⚖️ Your Rights

GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability – receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority
CCPA (California Users)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the "sale" of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in the Contact section below.

🏥 Health Regulations

HIPAA Notice: FitFlow is a general consumer wellness application and is not a covered entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Health information you enter is used solely to personalise your fitness experience within the app. While we apply HIPAA-aligned security safeguards (encryption, access controls, audit logging), this app does not constitute a medical service and your data is not protected health information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA.

Always consult a licensed physician or qualified healthcare professional before beginning any new exercise programme, especially if you have a pre-existing medical condition.

👶 Children's Privacy

FitFlow is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, applicable law, or our data practices. When we make material changes we will update the Effective Date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.

Your continued use of FitFlow after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
